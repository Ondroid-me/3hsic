THE
LANGUAGE
OF SOUND

Music is not merely sound organised in time. It is humanity's oldest living language — a grammar of emotion, a syntax of culture, a vocabulary that predates every written word. To learn music is to gain access to something older than memory and more universal than speech.

Once understood at its roots — theory, instrumentation, composition, performance — music becomes not just something you hear but something that reveals the character of everything you feel and put into the world.

"Music gives a soul to the universe, wings to the mind, flight to the imagination, and life to everything."

— Plato
